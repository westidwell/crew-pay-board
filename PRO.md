# Crew Pay Board Pro

The free board runs one crew on one phone. Pro is for when more than one person needs to see the numbers.

- **Crew sign-in**: Each crew member signs in on their own phone and sees only their own jobs and pay.
- **Office approval**: The office checks and approves each week before pay is final, and approved weeks lock.
- **Many crews and customer rate cards**: Run several crews from one place, with a separate rate card for each customer.
- **Photo proof**: Attach photos to each job as proof the work was done.
- **Payroll export**: Send approved weeks to payroll apps in the import format each one expects.
- **Pay history across years**: Year-to-date and year-end totals per person, with every past week a tap away.
